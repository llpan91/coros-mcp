## Analysis Rules

- Use these goals as the reference points for readiness assessments, training
  recommendations, and race projections.
- Treat the ladder as progressive: assess readiness for the next unachieved
  milestone on each distance rather than jumping to the hardest target.
- Do not mark a goal as achieved solely from device estimates or a shorter
  workout. Confirm it with an official race result or a sufficiently specific
  race-distance validation.
- Goal 0 is confirmed achieved by the 2026-09-13 official half marathon
  (1:38:23). All other goals remain active until confirmed by an official
  result at the required distance.
- For all training calculations, continue to follow `AGENTS.md`, including use
  of activity-detail `workoutTime` and verified full dates.
